    ITP quick reference

    One page — the anatomy, the numbering system, and the core reminders — meant to be open next to Remarkable, alongside DocAssist, while you're actually writing an ITP, not read start to finish.

    Before anything else: if you can't point to the ASAM sentence that justifies a goal, the goal doesn't belong in the plan yet. Every goal, objective, and intervention should trace back to something already documented.

    Three sections, matched to dimension pairs

    Section A — Dimensions 1 & 2 (e.g., Substance Use Disorders, Opioid Dependence, Chronic Pain)
    Section B — Dimensions 3 & 4 (e.g., PTSD, Anxiety, Depression, Family Conflicts, Treatment Resistance, and many more)
    Section C — Dimensions 5 & 6 (Relapse Proneness, Living Environment Deficiency, Parent-Child/Partner Relational Problems, Peer Group Negativity), plus an optional Case Management goal block
    Letters (A, B, C...) reset at the start of every section. A goal's real identity is Section + Letter together — "Section C, Goal A" is not the same goal as "Section A, Goal A."

    The three layers

    Goal: Picked from a category's preset options and edited, or written from scratch as a custom goal — never left as unedited library text. Gets a letter within its section. Never "completed" — it's a direction, not a checkbox. Requires a direct client quote. Carries one date: Established (auto-filled to today at generation).
    Objective: Checked off from the category's preset pairs, or added custom when nothing standard fits. Numbered to its goal's letter (1A, 2A, 1B...). Carries two dates: Start Date (auto-filled to today) and Target Date (the one field you actually choose).
    Intervention: Paired automatically with its objective when checked. Shares the objective's number/letter (Intervention 1A). Describes clinician action, not client behavior.

    Every goal, three tests

    • Traceable: does it answer a specific gap named in the ASAM, or could you not point to where it came from?
    • Individualized: has it been edited from the library default, and does it have a client quote attached?
    • Measurable: does the objective have a real Target Date (not "TBD") and a condition a reviewer could check off as done or not done?

    Quick gut-checks

    • If the objective could be true forever and never "complete," it's a goal wearing an objective's number.
    • If the intervention describes what the client will do, rewrite it as what the clinician will do.
    • Not every ASAM dimension needs a goal — write one where there's an active, unresolved need driving the current level of care.
    • DocAssist doesn't track review history — Established and Start Date just reflect the day you generate the plan. At review time, update the Target Date and document progress in the record yourself; the tool won't do it for you.

    Common ITP mistakes, at a glance

    Every pitfall from this module, gathered in one place.

    Anatomy
    Writing a goal where an objective belongs
    If it has no target date and could be true forever, it's a restated goal — not a measurable step.
    Sections
    Forgetting letters reset per section
    "Goal A" alone is ambiguous — always say which section. Section B's Goal A and Section C's Goal A are different goals.
    Objectives
    Leaving the Target Date at "TBD"
    "TBD" is really no target at all. Set a real date — even "next treatment plan review" works if that's a defined checkpoint.
    Interventions
    Describing what the client will do, not the clinician
    An intervention is your action — the technique or modality you'll use. The client's action belongs in the objective.
    Individualizing
    Accepting the library goal unedited, with no client quote
    This is exactly what triggers DocAssist's SMART warning and quote warning. Edit the text, and add her own words.
    Goal count
    A goal for every dimension, whether it needs one or not
    Write goals where there's an active, unresolved need — not as a box-checking exercise across all six dimensions.
    Reviews
    Assuming DocAssist tracks review history for you
    It doesn't — Established and Start Date just reflect the day you last generated the plan. If you regenerate a goal at a later review, its Established date will show that later day, not the original. Keeping track of history is on you and the record, not the tool.

    Frequently asked questions

    The questions staff actually ask about writing ITPs, answered directly. Click each one.

    + What if a client has a real need, but I'm not sure it rises to a whole goal?
    Not every true statement about a client needs its own goal — that's what the rest of the ASAM narrative is for. Ask: is this actively driving the need for this level of care right now? If yes, it earns a goal. If it's stable or resolving on its own, it may just need continued monitoring noted in the ASAM.
    + Do I have to use one of the library's goal categories?
    No. Every section has a "+ Add Custom Goal" option, letting you write the goal name, text, objectives, and interventions entirely from scratch. This isn't a workaround for edge cases — it's just as normal a path as the library, and often the right call when a client's situation doesn't map cleanly onto a preset category.
    + Can I just use one of the library's preset goal options as-is?
    You can start there, but it has to be edited before it's ready — DocAssist will flag a goal left exactly as the library wrote it with a SMART warning before you can submit. The library gives you a clinically sound starting point; making it true of this specific client is still your job.
    + Why does every goal need a client quote?
    It documents collaborative treatment planning — that the goal was built with the client, not just written about her. DocAssist will flag a goal that's missing one before you can submit.
    + What if none of the preset objective/intervention pairs fit?
    Add a custom one. This is a normal, expected part of the process — the library covers common ground, but a client's specific circumstances (like a specific person to avoid calling) sometimes need a pair written just for them.
    + What Target Date should I use if I'm not sure how long something will take?
    Pick the next treatment plan review date, or another real, defined checkpoint. Don't leave it at "TBD" — that's not a target, it's the absence of one.
    + If I regenerate an existing goal at a later review, does DocAssist remember when it was first written?
    No. Established and Start Date are always auto-filled to today, whenever you generate the output. If you regenerate a goal at a later review, its Established date will show that later day, not the original — so document the original date elsewhere in the record if it matters.
    + Do I need a separate goal for every ASAM dimension that has any rating above 0?
    No. A dimension can be actively monitored without a dedicated ITP goal if it isn't the thing driving continued need for this level of care. Goals belong to the findings doing the most clinical work.
    + Why is "Goal A" in Section B a different goal from "Goal A" in Section C?
    Letters reset at the start of every section, since each section is scoped to its own pair of dimensions. A goal's real identity is the section and the letter together — always reference both.
    + Who actually writes the intervention — me, or the client?
    You. The intervention is always the clinician's action — the specific technique or modality you'll use. What the client will do in response is the objective, one layer up.
